STELLA RUNNER · ORIGINAL PROCEDURAL AUDIO
All music and effects were synthesized for this game; no commercial recordings or melodies were sampled.
BGM: title (bright F-major adventure theme with a short fanfare, piano/plucked guitar lead, bell and string support; 24.8-second seamless loop), city (새벽 도심), forest (달빛 숲길), mine (수정 광산), sky (구름 위 하늘길).
The four stage cues have independently composed rhythms, harmonic plans, and melodies: bouncy steel guitar and piano; floating flute and harp in 3/4; French horn and strings for a climbing expedition; fast synth lead, electric guitar, bass and drums for cycling. They use the supplied game references as broad mood inspiration; melodies and arrangements are original.
Instrument timbres were rendered from TimGM6mb SoundFont by Tim Brechbill (GPL-2.0). The SoundFont itself is not included in this pack.
SFX: button, run, jump, double-jump, slide, hit, coin, shield, game-over, victory. Effects use a bright major-key pitch palette, bell/xylophone tones, soft marimba footsteps, airy slide and a playful boing.
Loop the five BGM files. Play sound effects as one-shots. Files are OGG Vorbis, mono 22.05 kHz.
These original files are provided for use with the Stella Runner prototype and its internal sharing.
