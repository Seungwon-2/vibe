# Plan: Coin Motif Pickup Sections

## Goal
Add readable, theme-shaped coin formations to both Endless and Story runs without coupling them to hazard patterns or increasing the normal coin economy. Endless receives one formation every 2,500m; each Story course receives one. The four motifs are fixed by biome: star, flower, music note, and cloud. Formations occupy verified safe windows and replace the ordinary safe-route coins in those windows; some points are elevated as optional jump rewards.
