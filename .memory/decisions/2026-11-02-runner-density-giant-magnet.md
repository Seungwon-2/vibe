# Decisions: Endless Density, Giant Pickup, and Mary's Magnet

- Date: 2026-11-02
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Endless course frequency
- **Chosen**: Raise density in Endless only. In the dense section beginning at 2,000m, reduce the base gap between hazards from 330m to 280m and the recovery gap from 605m to 515m. Reduce the safe-route coin target cadence from 90m to 75m. Preserve the progressive late-course tightening and its floors. Leave Story-authored hazard routes and Story safe-coin cadence unchanged.
- **Rationale**: Make the infinite run more engaging while retaining the Story course designs and giving the player distinct recovery windows after obstacle waves.

## D02. Giant pickup silhouette
- **Chosen**: Replace the round gold giant pickup with a clearly distinct purple-and-pink comet/star silhouette with a bright outline. Keep the standard coin round and gold-toned across biomes.
- **Rationale**: Let players distinguish the temporary giant effect from an ordinary coin at a glance.

## D03. Mary's magnetic passive
- **Chosen**: Replace Mary's current +50% pickup-body expansion with a visible magnet effect. In both Endless and Story, only Mary attracts coins and time pickups within a 150px radius toward the character over about 0.3 seconds, with a subtle magnetic sparkle. Giant pickups remain touch-collect only. Other characters' pickup behavior stays unchanged.
- **Rationale**: Make Mary's passive immediately visible and useful while keeping its scope and effect understandable.

## D04. Gameplay boundaries
- **Chosen**: Keep attraction and collectible visuals separate from hazard collision and runner motion. Attraction should not interrupt jump, double-jump, slide, or fall animation. Continue to enforce existing safe pickup placement against hazards, pits, and gates. Keep Story hazard positions, lengths, gates, and pit rules unchanged.
- **Rationale**: Improve item collection and Endless pacing without reintroducing the previous item-pickup animation interruption or unfair course overlaps.
